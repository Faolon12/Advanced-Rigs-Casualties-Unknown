Making a character rig

  1. Draw your character standing and facing right, at the game's scale (8 px = 1 unit; the game's character is about 50 px tall). Put every part on its own layer: head, chest, belly, each arm/leg piece (near and far side), and each tail/wing/hair piece you want to move.
  2. Open your .psd/.psb, or pick all your PNGs at once (one image per part is fine, cut out or full canvas). Parts are matched from names like Head, Chest, Belly, Shoulder Front, Arm Back, Hand, Thigh, Calf, Foot, Tail, Ear, Hair, Wing. Anything not recognised waits beside the character: pick its role in the dropdown on the left. Forgot something? Add images… (or drop more PNGs) adds them without starting over.
  3. Place: drag any part on the canvas (or use the arrow keys). The faint blue boxes are the game's own body parts your art rides on. Auto-align lines everything up at once.
  4. Bones: click your tail, wing, hair or ear. In the panel on the right, Make it move → choose what it is and how many bones → Add bones. One image is enough: it's cut into that many bendy pieces along its shape. Drag the yellow ● joints (where it bends) and the ◆ tip to fit, then Re-cut to bones. Change the number of bones any time.
  5. Test: pick an action (walk, run, crouch, jump, fall, climb, swim, attack, sleep, dead…) from the Action list. The body moves like it does in game and your animation frames for that action play. Grab the character with the mouse and shake it. Tweak the motion sliders until the tail/wings feel right.
  6. Export and unzip into BepInEx\plugins\AdvancedRigs\Rigs\. In game press F7 to pick it, F8 to reload after changes.

Tips

  • Any number of tail pieces works. Cut your tail into as many layers as you like (Tail1 = base).
  • Skins in the sprite-replacer format (experimentHead.png etc.) can be opened too; they're added as sprite swaps, including the jaw / disfigured / eye states.
  • Injury art: set a layer's role to Injury art to show it instead of your head when the game rips the jaw off etc.
  • Injurable parts (off by default): select a tail / wing / hair part and tick Can be injured. Each piece then works like a real limb: it can be hit, bleed, get infected, have shards pulled out, be bandaged, splinted, amputated, or torn off. Give pieces injury art (scratched, wounded, bandaged, stump…) by setting extra layers' role to Injury art, and place them on the Health screen tab.
  • Gear & skins: in game press F9 to save every sprite on your character (body, eyes, worn gear like backpacks and helmets, health screen) with its exact game name. Repaint them and load them with Add sprites…. Gear only changes while you wear it; everyone else (and the item on the ground) stays normal.
  • Hitboxes never change: your art rides on the game's own body parts.
