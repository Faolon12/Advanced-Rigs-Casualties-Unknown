# Advanced-Rigs-Casualties-Unknown
A mod for Casualties Unknown that lets you have custom rigs, with better springbones, etc, have a lonnngggg tail with 20 segments? Do it!, wings? No problem, optionally enable damage for them and they act like every other part! take damage, bandage them, you got glass shards in your tail? pull them out, watch out for the salad that want to eat your wings!
